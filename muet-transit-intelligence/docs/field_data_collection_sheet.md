# MUET Point Bus Field Data Collection Sheet (One-Page Protocol)

**Purpose:** Calibrate synthetic NHPP arrival rates `λ_k(t)` and corridor transit distributions using real student counts at **STC** and **Main Gate** across **3 representative weekdays** (e.g., Tue, Wed, Fri) during **Morning Peak (08:00–09:00)** and **Afternoon Peak (14:00–15:30)**.

## Observer Instructions
1. Stand at the designated queue entry point at **STC** or **Main Gate** with a synchronized digital clock and tally counter.
2. Record **newly arriving students** in each **5-minute bin** (do not double-count students already waiting from previous bins).
3. Record each **Point bus departure**: Bus ID, departure time, passengers boarded, and students left behind in queue (denied boarding).
4. Enter the averaged counts into the CSV template (`time_min,stc_count,mg_count`) and upload via **Configuration → Import Field CSV**.
